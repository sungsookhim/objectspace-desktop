# Code of Conduct

Objectspace follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in its issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else people take part in it.

In short: be welcoming and kind to everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity; disagree about ideas, never attack people; accept criticism of your work gracefully and give it with care; no harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour troubles you, write to the maintainer, Sungsoo Khim, at sungsookhim@gmail.com. Reports are read promptly and kept private. The maintainer decides what to do, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: from a private word, through a warning or a temporary ban, to a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
