# Version 0.6: plan (draft)

Draft, 2026-11-24, with [v0.6-design.md](v0.6-design.md). It plans the proposed 0.6 (the design's group A and suspend); if the owner keeps all nine parts in 0.6, steps 7 to 11 below follow the same way. Each step is usable on its own, ends with its tests passing (`make check`, `gui-test`, `comp-test`, the visual and golden checks) and CI green, and is checked by the owner on the VM before the next.

## Steps

1. **Start** (S): VERSION 0.6.0-dev; the owner's choices folded into the design.
2. **TheBar's trays** (M): trays as folders of shadows (`Trays/` in the user's data; a **Trays** folder in System); the first start makes **Default**; on the bar, the tray button and the current tray's icons; clicks, the tray menu (choose, add, rename, delete), drops onto the bar, **Remove from tray**; **Trays** on TheBar's Parts page. **Checks:** a headless test (`tests/comp/trays.sh`): the first tray shown, a click cycles, a drop adds a shadow, Remove takes it away, a new tray, rename, delete; the first-start test sees the folder.
3. **A menu bar in folder windows** (M): the menus from the folder's and the selection's menus as the service gives them (menus are data already); the **Menu bar** setting on the View page; Alt and F10 show it for a moment. **Checks:** a headless test: on in the notebook, the bar drawn, a menu opened by a click and by Alt; off, nothing; Selected follows the selection.
4. **The Column view** (M): the view in the widget set's container (columns of lists side by side, sideways scrolling, the path), the keys, the last column's details of a file. **Checks:** a GUI self-test (golden): three folders deep, the columns and the path; keys left and right; a file's details; live changes in a column.
5. **Thumbnails, the Preview setting and the Gallery view** (L): a thumbnail service in the shell (a worker, `glycin-thumbnailer` and the system's thumbnailers, the shared cache); **Show pictures**; the Gallery view. **Checks:** a fake thumbnailer in tests; a self-test: thumbnails in the Icon view, the cache written once and read again; the Gallery's moves; the visual baseline taken again where the views are shown.
6. **Suspend after idle** (S): GNOME's sleep settings read by the session; logind's Suspend after the delay, inhibitors honoured. **Checks:** a headless test with a fake logind on a private bus: suspend asked after the delay, not while a program inhibits.
7. **The release, 0.6.0** (S): the release notes; the RPMs in mock; the owner's install on a clean VM; the GitHub release.

If the screens and themes stay in 0.6: 8. suspend's step done, then **more than one screen** (L), **sharp X11 programs** (M), **GTK 3 theme and cursors** (L), **libadwaita's colours** (S), each with a headless test, before the release.

## Order and risks

- The trays and the menu bar first: the most visible, and they reuse what exists (folders of shadows as the Launcher; menus as data).
- **The Column and Gallery views** are the first views since 0.1; they change the widget set's container, so the existing views' golden and visual checks guard them.
- **Thumbnails** run other programs on the user's files: in a worker, with a time limit, and only installed thumbnailers.
- **More than one screen** touches most of the compositor (placement, the bar, the desktop, restoring windows); kept for its own release if the owner agrees.
